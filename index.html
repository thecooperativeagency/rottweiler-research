Original type targets

ADRK / FCI posture
Piece Target Red flag
Male height ~24–27 in (61–68 cm). ADRK “correct large” often ~65–66 cm. Sold as enormous novelty; structure sacrificed for bulk.
Male weight feel Powerful but mobile — often ~100–120 lb class when fit, not a brick. Regular 140–180 lb “guardian” marketing.
Build Compact, strong, endurance + power. Longer than tall slightly. Loose skin, barrel chest, draft-horse bone only.
Head Strong, proportional muzzle. Dark eyes/pigment. Serious expression. Smashed face, extreme stop, overdone cheek/skull.
Mind Calm, confident, biddable. Aloof with strangers, solid with family. Soft/shy, or hair-trigger “protection prospect” hype.
Proof German papers / clear pedigree + health + BH or breed suitability preferred. “German lines” with no Ahnentafel / no titles / no clearances.

AKC size band is similar on paper; culture and selection pressure differ. ADRK still polices breeding suitability hardest. USRC in the US tracks FCI/ADRK approach (BST, work + type).

Pedigree & health decoder

Read papers without getting sold
Health HD-A / HD-Frei

German hips clear (best). US parallel: OFA Excellent/Good.

Health ED-0 / ED-Frei

Elbows clear. US: OFA Normal elbows.

Health JLPP N/N

DNA clear. Also watch LEMP / NAD on modern stock. Avoid untested parents.

Breed worthiness ZTP

German breed suitability test — structure, type, temperament, drive.

Breed worthiness Zucht- u. Körfähig

Top German breeding clearance stamp on the Ahnentafel.

US parallel BST

USRC Breed Suitability Test — conformation + character/courage elements.

Work BH

Basic temperament + obedience bar. Minimum signal for serious lines.

Work IGP I–III

Real sport titles (old IPO/SchH). Fine in parents; III-only litters may be too hot for house companion.

Show (German) KS / V1

Klubsieger = ADRK national specialty winner. V1 = Excellent, first in class.

Papers Ahnentafel

Official ADRK pedigree. Titles + health often printed on it.

Clubs ADRK

German parent club — gold standard for breed rules and directory of German breeders.

Clubs USRC

US club aligned with FCI/ADRK culture. Events, BST, member referrals.

Companion-first selection

Green lights

  • German import or clear German / ADRK pedigree
  • HD-A or OFA Good/Excellent; elbows free/Normal
  • JLPP N/N both sides (or clear × carrier managed correctly — prefer clear)
  • BH minimum; optional light IGP I / ZTP / BST
  • Breeder places mostly family homes and matches temperament
  • Adult dogs look athletic and proportional in unedited photos/video

Yellow / red

  • Only IGP III protection marketing, no off-switch story
  • “Huge head / giant bone” as the selling point
  • Health claims without OFA links or German certs
  • Puppies always in stock, deposit before conversation
  • Cannot name recent family homes or share references

You’re not rejecting working blood. You’re rejecting sport-specialist intensity and commercial size drift.

From Baton Rouge — network & paths

Don’t expect deep ADRK bench locally

USRC clubs in reach

  • DFW Working Rottweilers — Plano, TX (events + people)
  • Central Florida Rottweiler Klub — Orlando area
  • Club directory: usrconline.org/usrc_clubs.html
  • USRC home: usrconline.org

One BST/show weekend will train your eye faster than fifty websites.

Three paths

  • A — Gold: ADRK-listed German kennel import. Directory on adrk.de.
  • B — Strong US: Documented German import / first-gen programs (this shortlist).
  • C — Young adult: Type + temperament already visible; sometimes smarter than a puppy.

Budget ballpark for serious German-type pup: often ~$3k–$5k+; imports/young titled stock can run more. Pickup / flight nanny beats mystery shipping mills.

24-month plan

No rush · type first
  1. Now — calibrate the eye Save adult photos from Tier 1 programs. Build a personal type board (side profile, front, moving).
  2. Next 3–6 months — touch the network Hit a USRC weekend (DFW or FL). Meet real dogs. Talk to handlers, not just websites.
  3. Inquiry wave Email 2–3 programs with the same brief (template below). Demand clearance links + adult video.
  4. Decide path US German-line puppy vs young adult vs true DE import. Logistics from BR matter.
  5. Only then — deposit After conversation, papers, and type you actually like. Walk if anything is vague.

Adult evaluation checklist

Print / screenshot this

Structure & type

  • Looks compact and athletic, not a meat slab
  • Muzzle length proportional (not smashed)
  • Clean topline / moves effortlessly on video
  • Male not sold primarily on extreme size
  • Both parents shown standing + moving

Health, mind, program

  • OFA or HD/ED numbers verified on official site
  • JLPP results for both parents
  • Pedigree / import story is documentable
  • BH or breed test preferred; temperament story clear
  • Recent family-home references available
  • Written contract + health guarantee + return clause

I’m in the Baton Rouge area and planning ahead for a male Rottweiler as a family guardian/companion. I will do serious obedience training. I am not looking for a high-drive sport-only prospect.

What I want:

Could you please share:
1) Current or upcoming litters and expected timing
2) Parent clearances (OFA links or German HD/ED + JLPP)
3) Adult photos/video of sire and dam (standing + moving)

Thank you — happy to jump on a call if useful.
